# End-wall and platform settings navigation — 22 September 2026

Moved End walls & gables and Platforms to top-level Component Settings accordions immediately after Roof. They are no longer nested inside Roof construction & trim. Each can expand independently while Roof stays collapsed.

For supported gabled parts without roof detailing, the groups remain visible and offer an explicit enable button. Opening the group alone does not change geometry. Existing settings and geometry are preserved. Current eligibility remains an enabled gable roof on a non-circular part.

Validation: 112 tests passed; production build passed with existing bundle warnings. In the isolated browser scene, verified both top-level accordions and expanded both while Roof remained collapsed.
